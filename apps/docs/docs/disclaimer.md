## No Financial Advice

Nothing in this documentation or the Zurpay application constitutes financial, legal, or tax advice. Consult qualified professionals for guidance on payroll, taxes, and regulatory compliance.

## Network Risks

### Solana Devnet

- Zurpay is deployed on **Solana Devnet** only
- Devnet tokens have **no monetary value**
- Devnet may be **reset or unavailable** at any time
- Do not send mainnet assets to devnet addresses

### Inco Lightning

- Inco Lightning is a **testnet/devnet** service
- FHE computation is performed by **third-party operators**
- Service availability is **not guaranteed**
- See [Trust Model](/resources/trust-model) for trust assumptions

## Smart Contract Risks

- Smart contracts may contain **bugs or vulnerabilities**
- Funds could be **lost or locked** due to contract errors
- Contract upgrades may **change behavior**
- The Zurpay program has **not been audited**

## Privacy Limitations

While Zurpay aims to provide confidential payments:

- **Transaction timing** is visible on-chain
- **Wallet addresses** are visible on-chain
- **Claim links** expose amounts to anyone with access
- **Service operators** may have access to data during processing

See [Trust Model](/resources/trust-model) for detailed analysis.
